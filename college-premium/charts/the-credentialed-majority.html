The Credentialed Majority

In 1992, about one in four American workers age 25 and up had a bachelor’s degree. Today it’s nearly one in two. The rise has been steady across every recession and every boom.

Method: Share of the U.S. civilian labor force age 25 and over holding a bachelor’s degree or higher, computed monthly from BLS Current Population Survey levels across all four education categories (less than high school, high school only, some college/associate degree, bachelor’s+). This is a companion to The Credential Gap, which charts unemployment across these same categories — this chart is the denominator shifting underneath that one.
